## Lesson 4

### Cost-effective AI solutions can provide enterprise learning opportunities.

Early in the project, a vendor-hosted AI platform was evaluated as the initial reference implementation. As the project evolved, the architecture transitioned toward a self-hosted open-weight LLM to reduce ongoing costs while improving flexibility, privacy, and understanding of AI infrastructure.

Selecting a locally hosted model allows the homelab to remain accessible without recurring subscription costs, while still providing hands-on experience with AI integration, automation, and cybersecurity workflows.

This decision also enables experimentation with different open-weight models without becoming dependent on a single vendor or platform.
